Reefer container: dimensions, door, pallet count

Insulation and the cooling unit make the reefer smaller inside than a dry box of the same length: 10 euro pallets per tier in the 20ft, 23 in the 40ft — and the tightest door of all standard builds.

The dimensions at a glance

All values in centimetres, as reference values — reefer series differ noticeably. They are the same values the 3D calculator stows with and checks the door against.

The 40ft reefer effectively always comes as a high cube build — hence the 242 cm internal height.

10 and 23 — calculated, not copied

In the dry box it is 11 and 25 euro pallets per tier. The reefer loses length and width inside (228 instead of 235 cm), and that costs: 10 in the 20ft, 23 in the 40ft — with turned pallets in the pattern the 3D calculator's packer lays. Keep every pallet in one orientation and it is only 8 and 18.

The tightest door — and the load line

About 228 × 218 cm of door opening: tall cargo that passes a GP door without a thought can get stuck at the reefer — the 3D calculator checks the reefer door on every stow. On top comes the cooling: cargo must stay below the red load line and needs air space above and below (T-bar floor) so the cold air can circulate.
