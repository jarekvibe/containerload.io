How many industrial pallets fit in a container?

Industrial pallets (100×120 cm) fit 9 into a 20ft and 22 into a 40ft container floor-loaded (one tier each). Stackable accordingly more.

In short

The industrial pallet (also called chemical pallet) measures 100×120 cm and is therefore slightly larger than the euro pallet. That is why fewer of them fit into the same container per tier.
