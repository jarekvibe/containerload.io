Container ports of the world: map and search

Where do container ships call? The map shows HK_ZAHL_EN ports with container handling. Search by name or UN/LOCODE, or pick a country and a coast — for example USA, North Pacific for the West Coast.

Ports: World Port Index (NGA), as of HK_STAND_EN · Map: Natural Earth

How to use the map

Drag to pan, use the mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom, and click a dot to see the port details. The search finds ports by name, by code (e.g. USLGB for Long Beach) or by country. Once you pick a country, the coasts and water bodies its ports lie on appear below — that is how you separate, say, the US West Coast (Pacific) from the East Coast (Atlantic) and the Gulf of Mexico.

Every selection is stored in the address bar. Share the link and the recipient sees the same selection.

Where the data comes from

The ports come from the World Port Index (Pub. 150) of the US National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, as of HK_STAND_EN. A port with container handling is one for which the index lists container facilities or container cranes. Harbour size, vessel length and depth are the index's figures; they are guide values and not complete for every port.

What the map does not show (yet): which carrier calls at which port, and how often. That schedule data is not freely and reliably available; we will only show it once it is right.
