How many euro pallets fit in a 20ft container?

A 20ft standard container holds 11 euro pallets floor-loaded (120×80 cm, one tier). With stackable goods accordingly more, limited by height and permitted weight.

In short

The internal length of a 20ft standard container is about 590 cm, the internal width about 235 cm. For euro pallets (120×80 cm) that works out to 11 floor positions in one tier.
