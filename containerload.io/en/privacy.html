2. Your entries and the calculator

Important: The load calculator runs entirely in your browser — the calculation itself happens on your device, no server takes part in it. There are exactly three cases in which details of your load are transmitted: the feedback form (section 6), which you trigger yourself and where you decide before sending whether the load goes along — the AI functions (section 7) — AI import, “AI email draft” and the loading assistant —, which transmit list text, a photo or the details of your plan only after your click — and the anonymous usage statistics (section 5), which transmit dimensions, weights and quantities without names and without any reference to a person. Your item names and any other free text never leave your device automatically — only in the feedback form and in the AI import, and there only through your click.

The load you entered last is also kept automatically in your browser's storage so that it is not lost when the page is reloaded. The same applies when you save a load under “My plans” or change the language, the unit of measurement, the carrier or the appearance (light/dark) (localStorage). A date is also stored there once the question “Did the plan work out?” has appeared — so that it does not come back. The checklists of the ramp mode (which loading steps you ticked off per plan) are kept only there as well, as are the container and seal numbers, the weights and the note you enter there under “Report loaded”. For the VGM declaration the calculator also remembers shipper, authorised person and place, so that you do not have to type them again for every container. In addition, the calculator remembers the version of shared plans it last saw (20 at most, each under a random plan identifier with no reference to a person), so that it can show what has changed the next time a link is opened. A loading report leaves your device only in the link you send yourself — containerload.io does not store it anywhere. The VGM declaration is created as a PDF file in your browser; it is not transmitted to any server either. All of this stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere. You can delete it at any time via your browser settings — or remove the load in the calculator with “Clear”.

4. Audience measurement (GoatCounter)

I use GoatCounter to measure visitor numbers. GoatCounter works without cookies, stores no personal data and no complete IP addresses. No individual can be identified. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in data-minimising audience measurement).

Besides page views I count a few anonymous events in the 3D calculator — for example that a load was entered, a plan calculated, shared or exported. Only the name of the event is transmitted (e.g. “plan-gerechnet”), never dimensions, weights, names or other details of your load. Each event is counted at most once per page view. The sole purpose is to see which functions are used.

5. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which loads the calculator needs to handle well, the 3D calculator transmits an anonymous summary to an endpoint of this website once per visit when the page is left: the dimensions, weights, quantities and stacking settings of the items entered, the container or vehicle chosen, the mode (sea/road), the language, the time spent and the names of the functions used (e.g. “excel-import”). On receipt, the country of origin of the request is noted as well (e.g. “DE”) — only the country, no city, no region.

Expressly not recorded: your item names and any other free text. Your IP address is not stored — on receipt, only the country code is derived from it, after which it is discarded. No identifier is set and no cookie is used. The events can be linked neither to one another nor to a person. Storage is with Netlify (section 3). If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control, nothing is transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the tool on the basis of actual use).

6. Feedback form

In the calculator there is a “Feedback” button at the bottom right, and after a plan has been created the question “Did the plan work out?” may appear once. Both open a form. Nothing is transmitted until you press “Send”.

What is then sent is exactly what the form shows: your rating, your free text, your email address for questions if you wish, and technical details for reproducing the case (container or truck chosen, number of items, window size, language, browser identification). If the box “Include the cargo” is ticked, a link containing the dimensions and weights you entered is sent as well — you can untick it before sending.

Transmission runs via the form system of Netlify (Netlify, Inc., 512 2nd Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA); from there the message is delivered to me by email. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent by sending) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the tool). I use an email address you leave solely to reply to the feedback and delete it as soon as the matter is settled.

7. AI functions (Anthropic Claude)

The AI import: The import dialog of the calculator has the buttons “Detect with AI” and “Photo / screenshot”. Nothing is transmitted until you press one of these buttons — or paste a screenshot into the input field yourself. Then the list text you pasted or the image you chose is transmitted, for recognising the load items, to a server of this website (Netlify, section 3) and from there to Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, San Francisco, California, USA), whose AI model “Claude” turns it into a suggested table — which you check and accept yourself. Only the text from the input field or the chosen image (reduced in size on your device before sending) is transmitted.

The “AI email draft” in the share dialog: If you press “Write the text” there, the details of your current plan — dimensions, weights, quantities, your item names, the equipment chosen and the share link — are transmitted to Anthropic in the same way so that “Claude” can draft an email from them. The draft appears in an editable field; whether and to whom you send it is entirely up to you.

The loading assistant (its name is Stevie): The button “Ask Stevie” opens a chat next to the plan. Each time a message is sent, your message, the previous course of this conversation and the details of your current plan (as with the AI email draft, including your item names) are transmitted. At your request, answers can carry out changes to the plan — they use the same controls as by hand, are immediately visible in the plan and can be taken back with “Undo” (Ctrl+Z). If an answer carries out such changes or a maximum check, up to two further requests with the freshly calculated figures of the same plan follow automatically so that the assistant can tell you and apply them — they belong to the one message you sent. The conversation lives only in the open page and is not stored.

For all these functions: I neither store nor evaluate your IP address in the process. According to its own statements, Anthropic does not use content transmitted via the programming interface to train its models and stores it only for a limited time (see Anthropic's privacy notice). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent by the click).
