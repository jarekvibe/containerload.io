How many wire mesh pallets fit in a container?

A 20ft container holds around 11 wire mesh pallets floor-loaded, a 40ft container around 23 (one tier). Because mesh pallets are stackable, more fit if height and payload allow.

In short

The standardised wire mesh pallet (Gitterbox) measures around 124×83 cm externally — four centimetres longer and three wider than a euro pallet (120×80 cm). In a 20ft container that makes no difference: 11 units, same as euro pallets. In a 40ft container it does: 23 instead of 25, because one box is lost in each row.

Stacking gets more in

Wire mesh pallets are designed to be stacked (usually 3–4 high, depending on load rating). Whether that pays off depends on weight — heavy boxes hit the payload limit before the height is used up. Just enter your unit weight in the calculator: it stacks automatically and warns when the payload is exceeded.
