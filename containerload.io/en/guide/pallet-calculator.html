Pallet calculator: how many pallets fit in a container or truck?

A 20ft container fits 11 euro pallets or 9 standard pallets (48 × 40 in), a 40ft 25 or 20 – single-tier. Stacked two high the figures double, if height and payload allow. The calculator below works out any pallet for every container and truck at once.

Why not just divide the floor area by the pallet area? Because the width decides: a container is 235 cm (92.5 in) wide inside. Two euro pallets lengthwise (160 cm) leave 75 cm – too little for a third. Only a mix of lengthwise and crosswise fills the floor; the reasoning is on the page about floor positions in a container.

The difference is large: pallets 125 cm (49 in) high stand single-tier in a standard container but two high in a high cube – twice as many in the same 40ft slot. If pallets go in already stacked, the stack must also pass the door, which is lower than the interior (see container dimensions). And only what can carry the load on top is stackable.

Weight: when the payload runs out first

With small vehicles the limit is often the weight, not the space. A 7.5-t truck takes 12 euro pallets on the floor but carries around 2,800 kg – at 250 kg per pallet that is 11. A van with around 1,200 kg payload takes 4 instead of 6 at 250 kg each. Enter the weight above and the calculator shows where the payload runs out first. For containers, what is allowed on the road also counts: weight limits by country.

Frequent questions

How many pallets fit in a 20ft container?
11 euro pallets or 9 standard pallets (48 × 40 in) single-tier. Stacked, twice that, if each pallet is at most 119 cm (46.9 in) high and the weight allows.

How many pallets fit in a 40ft container?
25 euro pallets or 20 standard pallets single-tier. In a 40ft high cube, stacked twice that, with pallets up to 135 cm (53.1 in) high.

How many pallets fit on a truck?
A curtainsider takes 34 euro pallets or 26 standard pallets single-tier, a 7.5-t truck 12 and a van 6 euro pallets.

How high can a pallet be to stack two high?
In a standard container at most 119 cm (46.9 in), in a high cube and on a curtainsider 135 cm (53.1 in) – based on internal heights of 239 and 270 cm.
